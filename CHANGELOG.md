# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — first release

Cave Aurora Edge for the CORSAIR XENEON EDGE, with its free companion bridge.

- **Shortcuts.** Up to eight programs, folders or websites, four on screen at a time.
  Pick a program in a normal Windows file box and it is added straight away, or paste a
  path or a web address. Each tile shows the program's own icon; REARRANGE changes the
  order by tapping two tiles.
- **PC controls.** Lock in one tap. Shut down and Restart need a press and hold until the
  ring fills, so a stray touch cannot end your session. Task Manager is one tap.
- **Focus and tasks.** Due dates, durations, priorities and suggestions from past tasks.
  Press play on a task and its length becomes the goal on the timer; pause, take timed
  breaks, and every stretch of work is saved with the time against it. A calendar shows
  which days have work, deleting asks first and offers Undo, and a reminder appears when
  it is time for a break.
- **Countdown timer.** Named, any length, with snooze. Stored by its finishing time, so it
  survives restarts, sleep and iCUE reloads.
- **Media and sound.** Title, artist, app and artwork, a draggable progress bar, transport
  controls, a volume slider sized for a finger, and a switch between apps when two are
  playing. Mute, mic mute, Switch Sound and a picker for any output on your PC.
- **System readings.** CPU, GPU, RAM and network along the bottom with history graphs. CPU
  and GPU work as soon as the widget is added, from iCUE's own sensors, on Intel or AMD
  and any graphics card. Each reading has a source setting in iCUE, and a reading with no
  source says where to fix it.
- **Layout.** Tap LAYOUT and drag one panel onto another to swap them. Every panel keeps
  its own size wherever it is put.
- **Colours.** Five backgrounds — Midnight Aurora, Ember Aurora, Total Eclipse, Violet
  Nebula and Graphite Slate — with any accent and text colour, set in iCUE's Widgets
  settings. Every change shows straight away, and every combination is checked for
  readability before it is applied. Background transparency fades the background, never
  the text.
- **Options.** 12 or 24-hour clock, Movie Mode brightness, alarm volume, work goal, break
  length and break reminders, and a reset that asks twice.
- **Also.** Movie Mode dims the whole dashboard, Show desktop, Windows notifications with
  a count, a button that switches the XENEON EDGE display mode, and swiping left or right
  to change iCUE screen.

### Companion bridge 1.0.5

A small Windows program that starts with Windows and listens only on your own PC. It
powers the sound controls, media details, RAM and network readings, the notification
count, the shortcuts and the PC controls. Nothing is sent to the internet, and there is
no account and no telemetry.
